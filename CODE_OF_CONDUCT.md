# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Pramaan to be a place where anyone can help fight financial fraud —
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, caste, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this project:

- Being kind and patient with first-time contributors.
- Giving technical feedback about the code, not the person.
- Assuming good faith, and asking a question before assuming a mistake.
- Accepting that "no" is a valid answer when a feature conflicts with the
  offline / no-telemetry promise.
- Respecting privacy: never pasting a real victim's message, phone number, VPA or
  account details into an issue, PR, screenshot or test.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Discriminatory jokes or comments, including about caste, religion or region.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Deliberately shipping a detector bypass, a working scam script aimed at real
  victims, or malware.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **omprakashsuthar@users.noreply.github.com**.
All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove
comments, close issues, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors.

Consequences escalate with severity and repetition:

1. A private warning and a request to stop.
2. A temporary ban from interaction.
3. A permanent ban from the project.
