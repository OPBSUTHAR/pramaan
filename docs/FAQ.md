# Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Pramaan need the internet?**
No. The engine runs entirely on-device. No network calls are made during a scan.

**Which languages are supported?**
Hindi and English. More regional languages are planned (see ROADMAP.md).

**Can I add a new scam template?**
Yes — see CONTRIBUTING.md. A template needs a test proving it scores at least `dangerous`.

**Is my data stored?**
No. Nothing is uploaded. Scans stay on your device.

**How do I report a bug?**
Open an issue. Never paste a real victim's message, phone number, VPA or account number — rewrite with placeholders.
